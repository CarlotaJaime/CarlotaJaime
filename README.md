## Carlota Jaime Barquero

I am a Microbiologist and Bioinformatician graduated from the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB), currently in the first year of my PhD at the Germans Trias i Pujol Research Institute (IGTP).  
My research focuses on the genomic epidemiology of nosocomial bacteria using long-read sequencing. My main objective is to reconstruct plasmid and other mobile genetic elements to decipher clonal dissemination and horizontal gene transfer dynamics.  
Additionally, I-m focused in exploring the virulence of clinical enterococci, specifically _Enterococcus faecalis_ and _Enterococcus faecium_, linking hybrid assemblies with clinical metadata and _in vivo_ infection models.

This work aims to provide robust scientific evidence on the incremental value of ONT in hospital genomic surveillance.
